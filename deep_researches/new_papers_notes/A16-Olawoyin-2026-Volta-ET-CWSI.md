# Remote Sensing-Based Assessment of Evapotranspiration and Crop Water Stress in the Volta Basin, 2000–2023

## Summary
Spatiotemporal assessment of land surface temperature (LST), evapotranspiration (ET), and the Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI) over the Volta River Basin (West Africa), 2000–2023, using MODIS, CHIRPS, and ESA satellite products. CWSI is estimated with an **LST–NDVI triangular (trapezoidal) approach**. Results: basin-wide warming (strongest in the south), persistent vegetation decline in northern/central zones (NDVI range −0.143 to 0.769), and high crop-water stress in the north (mean CWSI ≈ 0.975; range 0.955–0.996). ET correlates strongly with rainfall (r ≈ 0.99) and NDVI (r ≈ 0.96) and negatively with CWSI (r ≈ −0.96). Land-cover change: wetlands −74.76%, cropland −51.50%, built-up +58.05% — amplifying thermal and hydrological stress.

## Methods / Approach
- MODIS + CHIRPS + ESA products, 2000–2023 multi-decadal archive
- CWSI from LST–NDVI triangular space (no in-situ data required)
- Correlation analysis (ET vs rainfall/NDVI/CWSI) + land-cover change detection

## Key quantitative results
- Northern CWSI mean ≈ 0.975 (0.955–0.996)
- r(ET, rainfall) ≈ 0.99; r(ET, NDVI) ≈ 0.96; r(ET, CWSI) ≈ −0.96
- Land cover 2000–2023: wetlands −74.76%, cropland −51.50%, built-up +58.05%

## Relevance to thesis
A concrete multi-decadal, in-situ-free CWSI workflow (LST–NDVI triangle) usable as an **auxiliary water-stress covariate/validator** for the Khuzestan analysis where ground CWSI is sparse — analogous to the TVDI approach already noted in phase1. Demonstrates coupling of stress metrics with land-cover trajectories (cropland loss, built-up expansion) — relevant as environmental covariates for the sugarcane landscape. Arid/semi-arid marginal-zone framing parallels Khuzestan's water-scarcity context, though at much coarser (MODIS) resolution.

## Gaps / opportunities
- MODIS-scale (250–1000 m) — no field-scale or intra-field analysis
- Water stress only; no salinity dimension despite irrigated basin agriculture
- Correlational, not predictive: no ML model, no uncertainty quantification
