## Summary
This survey organizes LLM-powered spatial intelligence across scales—from embodied agents to smart cities to Earth science. It maps the progression from spatial memory and understanding toward reasoning and intelligence, grounding the discussion in cognitive science. It reports no new experiments.

## Methods / Architecture
- Multi-scale taxonomy: embodied → urban → Earth spatial intelligence
- Progression: spatial memory/understanding → reasoning/intelligence
- Cognitive science grounding
- Literature synthesis only (no new experiments)

## Key quantitative results
- No new experimental results (position/survey contribution)
