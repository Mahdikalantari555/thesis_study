## Summary
This position paper argues that generic agentic AI fails on Earth observation (EO) because EO data are georeferenced, multi-modal, and temporally structured, and because agent pipelines exhibit silent error propagation. It proposes design principles—structured geospatial state, tool-aware reasoning, verifier-guided execution, validity-aware learning/evaluation—and catalogs EO foundation models (Galileo, CROMA, SatMAE, Prithvi-2, TerraMind) and agents (RS-Agent, ThinkGeo, REMSA, OpenEarthAgent).

## Relevance to thesis
Provides conceptual justification for EO-native agents over generic LLM agents; critical framing for multi-temporal crop-stress series where silent error propagation across steps is a primary risk.

## Gaps / opportunities
- Design principles need concrete instantiation for agricultural stress workflows
- Opportunity to operationalize verifier-guided execution against AgroBench/Earth-Bench-Pro
- Multi-temporal validity checks for stress indices remain an open engineering target
