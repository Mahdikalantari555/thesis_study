# Study note — Carsaroli et al. 2020

## Citation (verified via Crossref, 2026-11-11)

Carsaroli, A., et al. (2020). Water deficit effects on sugarcane canopy temperature and spectral reflectance. African Journal of Crop Science 14(3):400-407. DOI: 10.21475/ajcs.20.14.03.p1647

PDF: `D:\RS\Thesis\New_papers\carsaroli_14_3_2020_400_407.pdf` (downloaded)

## Key findings relevant to this thesis

- Documents water deficit stress signatures in sugarcane using canopy temperature and spectral vegetation indices
- Relevant for P1 perturbation catalogue: NDVI drop under water deficit stress during mid-season (Jul–Sep)
- Provides physiological basis for linking NDVI decline to water stress in sugarcane

## Methods used

- Canopy temperature measurements (infrared thermometry)
- Spectral reflectance measurements (NDVI and related vegetation indices)
- Water deficit treatments applied at different growth stages
- Correlation analysis between canopy temperature, NDVI, and yield

## Findings-13 implications

- Relevant for P1: NDVI drop (water deficit, mid-season, Jul–Sep, gradual, 30–60 d, partial recovery) — KEEP
- Relevant for OQ6: advisor question on realistic stress signatures (pre-harvest irrigation cut-off, waterlogging occurrence)
- Supports the water+salinity stress focus of this thesis (Khuzestan sugarcane)
