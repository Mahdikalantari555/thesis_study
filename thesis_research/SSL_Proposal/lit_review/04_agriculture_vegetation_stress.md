# 04 — SSL for Agriculture and Vegetation Stress: Evidence Audit

**Central question:** does the literature support the claim that SSL-derived representations improve vegetation *stress* modeling? We separate (A) direct evidence (stress/anomaly detection from SSL features), (B) indirect evidence (crop/land-cover/irrigation via SSL), and (C) supervised stress-ML context. Evidence levels: [FT] full text; [ABS] abstract; [MO] metadata.

**Bottom line (supported by the verified corpus):** SSL demonstrably improves *label efficiency* for agricultural classification tasks; SSL-based *anomaly detection* on SITS is emerging (2024–2026) but rests on a handful of preprints; **no verified study performs label-free SSL anomaly detection validated against independent, field-level physiological ground truth for water or salinity stress on a public satellite archive** — this remains an open position.

---

## A. Direct evidence — SSL for stress / anomaly detection

| Study | Status | What it actually did | Ground truth quality | What it proves / does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **STS-NET** (Dalal et al., arXiv:2607.18791, 2026) [FT, local] | preprint | 3D-CAE SSL on PlanetScope VI time series (T=32); frozen features → RF/XGBoost classifiers | **Controlled stress experiment**: 62 sugarcane plots, Lakhimpur-Kheri, water × N treatments (9 classes) — experimentally imposed stress, not natural field stress | Water-stress precision 97.98%, N 85.08%, combined 83.47% [FT]. Proves SSL features carry stress-discriminative signal under controlled conditions. Does **not** prove label-free detection: final typing needs labels. Indices-only input (no LST); 2.5-acre single test plot |
| **EigenCL** (Ahmad, Brandt; arXiv:2506.03394 + extended arXiv:2608.25888; same work) [FT, local] | preprints | Contrastive embedding of Sentinel-2 **NDRE trajectories**; eigenvector-guided similarity (biology-grounded positives); 4 stress clusters, label-free staging | **Multi-source indirect validation**: soil-moisture records, US Drought Monitor, county yield statistics; cross-year transfer (Iowa 2020 → Nebraska 2023, no retraining) [FT] | Silhouette 0.748 / DBI 0.35 / CHI 49,624; severe-stress cluster peaks at VT–R1; early detection up to 12 days before NDRE thresholds; clusters correlate with soil moisture. Proves label-free *staging* is feasible and physiologically coherent. Does not: field-level ECe/water-stress ground truth; single crop (maize); single index (NDRE); author-affiliated preprint, not peer-reviewed |
| **Sadbhave et al.** (arXiv:2507.13514, 2025) [FT, local] | preprint/workshop-style | Fully unsupervised 3D-CAE + acquisition-date temporal encodings on Sentinel-2 SITS; downstream clustering separates stressed vs healthy sugar-beet fields | Not extracted in depth (intro verified); multi-year applicability claimed | Proves the reconstruction→clustering pipeline runs end-to-end label-free across years. Quantitative stress-validation evidence thin |
| **Label-free MIM aerial AD** (Anupam & Sobti, Plaksha; arXiv:2404.08931, 2024) [FT, local] | preprint | MAE on UAV agricultural imagery with an **anomaly-suppression loss** (trains on contaminated data without separating "normal" samples); Agriculture-Vision benchmark | Agriculture-Vision anomaly masks (annotated irregularities: nutrient deficiency, water stress, etc.) | +6.3% mIoU over prior SOTA [FT]. Proves masked reconstruction yields a usable per-pixel anomaly score *and* a practical answer to contaminated training sets. Single-date aerial images — no temporal dimension |
| **Temporal inpainting AD** (Rouet-Leduc & Hulbert; arXiv:2512.23986, 2025) [FT, local] | preprint | SATLAS-based model reconstructs the last S2 frame from preceding acquisitions; prediction–observation discrepancy = anomaly score; globally distributed training | Earthquake surface ruptures (2023 Turkey–Syria) vs temporal median & Reed–Xiaoli detectors | Detection threshold ~3× lower than baseline detectors [FT]. Proves temporal-redundancy reconstruction detects *surface change* with high sensitivity. **Not crop stress** — but the exact mechanism proposed for the thesis's temporal reconstruction arm |
| **Embeddings-AD for crop reference data cleaning** (VITO/ESA; arXiv:2607.23908, 2026) [ABS] | preprint | SSL embeddings flag bad labels in WorldCereal reference data | Label-quality proxies | Agricultural *label* QA, not stress detection — evidence that SSL embeddings act as reliable normality references in agri data pipelines |

**Synthesis of direct evidence.** All six are 2024–2026 items, five preprints. Reconstruction-error and trajectory-contrastive anomaly scores are the two demonstrated mechanisms; contaminated-normal training is addressed once (MIM-AD's suppression loss) and cross-year transfer is demonstrated once (EigenCL). None uses thermal/LST channels; none is on Landsat; none disentangles water vs salinity stress; none couples the anomaly score to physiological measurements (sap flow, gs, canopy temperature *in situ*) rather than proxy products.

## B. Indirect evidence — SSL for crop classification / irrigation / land cover

1. **Crop-type classification benefits from RS-SSL weights at low label counts.** Chang et al. (IGARSS 2025, arXiv:2409.09451) [FT]: SSL4EO-S12 pretraining beats ImageNet/SatlasPretrain by 3–27% F1 across five continents; 100 labeled images suffice for high overall accuracy, 900 needed to overcome class imbalance for average accuracy. CROMA's Canadian Cropland benchmark (crop types: barley, canola, corn…) shows SSL features rank first under kNN/K-means (76.06 K-means on EuroSAT-type tasks; Can. Crop kNN 61.25) [FT, Tables 1–2].
2. **Few-shot benchmark:** EuroCropsML (2026, DOI 10.1016/j.ophoto.2026.100117) [ABS]: SSL beats from-scratch and transfer learning for fine-grained crop-type features; MAML slightly higher but costlier; supervised transfer works best on geographically close regions.
3. **Irrigation detection without labels:** Agastya et al. 2021 [FT, local; venue not independently verified — workshop-era preprint]: contrastive SSL on optical imagery detects irrigation with "up to nine times better precision, 90% better recall and 40% more generalization" than supervised baselines [FT abstract]. Water-*use* detection, not water-*stress*.
4. **SSL features encode environmental/biological variables:** geospatial SSL representations probed against environmental signals (arXiv:2607.05207) [ABS]; below-ground fungal biodiversity predicted from SSL satellite features (arXiv:2604.09818, Cambridge) [ABS]. Evidence for signal richness, not stress validation.
5. **Field-boundary/agri mapping with temporal pretexts:** Time2Agri FF 54.2% IoU national-scale delineation [FT]; weakly-supervised SITS crop mapping at 95% of fully-supervised performance (Exact, CVPR 2025; DOI 10.1109/cvpr52734.2025.01310) [ABS].
6. **Drought datasets (non-SSL):** DroughtSet (arXiv:2412.15075) [FT, local] provides CONUS S2S drought-index forecasting benchmarks — usable as *evaluation* infrastructure for SSL drought representations, but itself supervised forecasting.

**What indirect evidence licenses:** SSL features are label-efficient and transferable for agri tasks with *discrete, stable* targets (crop type, irrigated vs rainfed, field boundaries). Stress is different in kind: continuous, episodic, confounded (water vs nutrients vs salinity vs disease), and only weakly visible in optical indices.

## C. Supervised stress-ML context (the comparison set SSL must beat)

- **Feature-optimized ensemble ML on EVI** (Elbeltagi et al., 2026; corpus #79) [FT, local]: 8 years of MODIS products (LAI, Fpar, ESI, NDVI, LST as predictors) for vegetation water stress in the Nile Delta; train 2018–2023 / independent test 2024–2025 — the temporal-split evaluation protocol the thesis should imitate.
- **DL plant-stress review** (Paul et al., *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* 229 (2025) 109734; DOI 10.1016/j.compag.2024.109734) [FT, local]: systematic review; cautions that image augmentation choices (color jitter) can destroy stress-diagnostic signal — relevant to SSL augmentation policy.
- **CWSI/thermal baselines** (corpus #10, #13, #25, #36, #37, #39, #42, #75, #76; e.g., Zoratipour Sentinel-2-vs-Landsat CWSI): the operational baseline — index thresholds from LST/NDVI. Any SSL anomaly score must be compared against these cheap baselines; the verified SSL corpus contains **no such comparison**.
- **Combined water–salinity stress RS** (corpus #69–71, Wen et al.): remote disentangling of the two stresses is hard even with supervision; no SSL work attempts it.
- **Quality caveat:** "Deep learning-based anomaly detection for precision field crop protection" (Wei, Shan, Zhen; Frontiers in Plant Science 2025; fpls-16-1576756) [FT, local] — multimodal field AD framework; retained for completeness but flagged: generic framing and author backgrounds suggest low review stringency; treat as weak evidence only.

## D. Answering the brief's questions explicitly

1. **Does SSL improve vegetation stress modeling?** Not yet demonstrated as a verified claim. Demonstrated: SSL features detect *imposed* stress (STS-NET, controlled), *stage* drought stress label-free with proxy validation (EigenCL), and detect generic field anomalies (MIM-AD). Unproven: label-free detection of *natural* field water/salinity stress validated against independent physiological ground truth.
2. **When/under what conditions?** The evidence clusters where (i) stress has a strong, monotonic spectral-temporal signature (drought → NDRE/NDVI decline), (ii) temporal trajectories rather than single dates are used, (iii) validation uses multi-source proxies (soil moisture, drought maps, yield) rather than labels alone.
3. **Is anomaly detection = stress detection?** No — flagged explicitly. Temporal-inpainting AD detects *any* surface change (earthquakes [FT]); MIM-AD detects *any* field irregularity; EigenCL's clusters are stress-*staged* but validated against drought proxies only. Conflating these would overclaim the thesis's contribution.
