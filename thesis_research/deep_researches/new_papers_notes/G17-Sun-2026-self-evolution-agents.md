## Summary
The paper proposes a Need-Driven Self-System combined with reinforcement-learning (RL) based intelligent agent self-evolution for label-efficient remote sensing detection, demonstrated on SAR ship detection. The approach aims to remain competitive without large annotated label sets. Exact mAP values are not stated in the first-page extract.

## Methods / Architecture
- Need-Driven Self-System framework.
- RL-driven intelligent agent self-evolution.
- Application domain: SAR ship detection (label-efficient RS detection).

## Key quantitative results
- Approach reported as competitive without large label sets.
- Exact mAP and comparative scores are not provided in the first-page extract; verify against the full PDF.

## Relevance to thesis
Contributes to the label-efficient autonomous RS detection literature: self-evolving agents that reduce annotation demand are conceptually aligned with active learning for arid crop-stress monitoring, though the paper is not crop-stress specific.

## Gaps / opportunities
- Domain is SAR ship detection, not agricultural stress phenotyping.
- No direct integration with EO foundation models or active-learning acquisition functions.
- Opportunity: adapt need-driven self-evolution loops to stress-label acquisition under EO-FM embeddings.
