## Summary
This is the first dedicated survey of agentic AI in remote sensing. It develops a taxonomy spanning single-agent copilots versus multi-agent orchestrators, covering planning, RAG, and memory. It reviews RS foundation models (SatMAE, RingMo), multimodal LLMs (GeoChat, LHRS-Bot, RS-LLaVA, SkySenseGPT, RingMoGPT), and agentic systems (RS-Agent, GeoAgent, Change-Agent, MapBot), and reframes evaluation from pixel accuracy toward trajectory-aware reasoning correctness.

## Methods / Architecture
- Taxonomy: single-agent copilots vs multi-agent orchestrators
- Component analysis: planning, RAG, memory
- Survey of RS FMs: SatMAE, RingMo
- Survey of MLLMs: GeoChat, LHRS-Bot, RS-LLaVA, SkySenseGPT, RingMoGPT
- Survey of agents: RS-Agent, GeoAgent, Change-Agent, MapBot

## Key quantitative results
- No new experiments; qualitative taxonomy and literature synthesis
- Evaluates shift from pixel accuracy to trajectory-aware reasoning correctness

## Relevance to thesis
Provides the conceptual map for agentic RS, foundation models, and an evaluation philosophy aligned with process-aware (not only outcome-level) assessment of crop-stress reasoning agents.

## Gaps / opportunities
- Agri and crop-stress applications remain thin within the taxonomy
- Opportunity to situate crop-stress agents within the multi-agent + memory branch of the survey
- Trajectory-aware metrics need operationalization for temporal stress series
