# GeoMMBench and GeoMMAgent: Toward Expert-Level Multimodal Intelligence in Geoscience and Remote Sensing

**Authors:** Xiao, A., Cheng, S., Xu, Y., Ren, Y., Chen, H., & Yokoya, N.  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** arXiv  
**DOI / arXiv:** arXiv:2604.08896v1  
**Local PDF:** 2604.08896v1.pdf  
**Type:** methods  
**Thesis relevance:** Medium  

## Summary
The paper introduces GeoMMBench, a benchmark of 1,053 expert-level multimodal multiple-choice questions across multi-sensor RS data, and evaluates 36 MLLMs. It also proposes GeoMMAgent, a multi-agent system with Retrieval + Perception + Reasoning stages augmented by domain RS tools, reporting significant gains over standalone LLMs.

## Methods / Architecture
- GeoMMBench: 1,053 expert MCQs across multi-sensor geoscience/RS data
- Evaluation of 36 MLLMs
- GeoMMAgent: Retrieval + Perception + Reasoning multi-agent pipeline
- Domain-specific RS tools integrated into agent loop
- Project: geo-mm-agi.github.io

## Key quantitative results
- 1,053 expert MCQs; 36 MLLMs evaluated
- Significant gains for GeoMMAgent over standalone LLMs (exact numbers not in extract)

## Relevance to thesis
Demonstrates multi-sensor expert RS reasoning agents and provides a benchmark pattern applicable to multimodal crop-stress diagnosis across optical/SAR inputs.

## Gaps / opportunities
- Exact GeoMMAgent gains need full-PDF verification
- MCQ format may not transfer directly to open-ended stress-trajectory reasoning
