## What It Covers

Comprehensive survey of self-supervised learning (SSL) methods including:
- Contrastive learning (SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL)
- Masked autoencoding (MAE, BEiT)
- Clustering-based approaches (DeepCluster, SwAV)
- Predictive methods

## Key Takeaway for Thesis

Provides methodological foundation for SSL pre-training strategy. Your thesis can leverage contrastive learning or masked autoencoding approaches from this review for pre-training the CNN-Mamba backbone on unlabeled SITS data.
