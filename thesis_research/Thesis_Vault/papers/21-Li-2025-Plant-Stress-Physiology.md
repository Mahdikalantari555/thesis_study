## Key Physiological Findings

### Stress Signaling Cascade

```
Stress perception → Electrical signals (E) + Ca²⁺ waves + ROS → 
Transcriptional reprogramming → Hormone signaling → 
Growth-defense tradeoff → Phenotypic response
```

### Key Hormones in Stress Response

| Hormone | Role in Stress | Cross-talk with Other Stresses |
|---------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **ABA** (Abscisic acid) | Primary drought/salinity signal; stomatal closure | Antagonizes GA; synergizes with ET for stress |
| **JA** (Jasmonic acid) | Defense signaling, salt tolerance | Antagonizes SA; modulates root growth |
| **SA** (Salicylic acid) | oxidative stress response | Antagonizes JA; primes systemic resistance |
| **ET** (Ethylene) | Stress adaptation, senescence | Interacts with ABA for stomatal regulation |
| **BR** (Brassinosteroid) | Growth maintenance under stress | Synergizes with auxin for cell expansion |
| **CK** (Cytokinin) | Cell division, stress senescence delay | Antagonizes ABA; promotes growth |

### Critical Finding: Interactive Effects

> "Crop resilience in the face of stress depends upon the speed and effectiveness with which plants and cropping systems sense and respond to that stress."

The paper documents that **combined stresses produce non-additive effects** because:
1. Hormonal pathways intersect (e.g., ABA for drought interacts with SOS pathway for salinity)
2. Resource allocation tradeoffs (growth vs. defense) are compounded
3. ROS production under dual stress exceeds individual stress responses
