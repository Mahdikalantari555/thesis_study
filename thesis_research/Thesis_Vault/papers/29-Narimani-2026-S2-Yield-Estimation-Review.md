# 29 — Sentinel-2 for Crop Yield Estimation: Systematic Review (Narimani et al., 2026)

**Full Citation:** Narimani, M., Pourreza, A., Moghimi, A., & Farajpoor, P. (2026). Sentinel-2 for Crop Yield Estimation: A Systematic Review. *arXiv:2603.23779*.  
**Status:** Read

---

## What It Covers

Systematic review of 301 peer-reviewed papers (2015–present) on Sentinel-2 based crop yield estimation.

## Three Primary Methodological Approaches

1. **Empirical models**: VI + ML/DL (Random Forest, CNN) — consistently outperform traditional regression
2. **Process-based models**: WOFOST, SAFY coupled with data assimilation of LAI and other biophysical variables
3. **Data fusion**: Sentinel-2 optical + Sentinel-1 SAR to overcome cloud cover limitations

## Key Findings

- **Geographic trend**: Research expanded from Europe/North America to global coverage
- **Crop focus**: Wheat, maize, rice, soybean dominate; sugarcane underrepresented
- **Performance**: Within-field yield variability well-explained by S2-based ML/DL
- **Limitations**: Limited/noisy ground-truth data, cloud gaps in optical imagery, cross-year/site transfer difficulties

## Relevance to Thesis

**Low-Medium** — State-of-the-art context for S2 yield estimation:
- ✅ Comprehensive S2 methodology overview
- ✅ Documents performance trends and limitations
- ⚠️ Yield-focused, not stress-focused
- ❌ No stress disentanglement angle
- ❌ No temporal XAI discussion
- ❌ Not sugarcane-specific

## Key Gap Identified

Most S2 yield studies focus on temperate cereals; tropical crops (especially sugarcane) remain understudied despite high commercial importance.
