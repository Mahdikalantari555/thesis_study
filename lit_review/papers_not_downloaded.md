## Access Tips

- For papers behind a paywall: try **institutional VPN**, then publisher DOI link.
- For arXiv papers: just click the `arXiv:xxxx.xxxxx` link — free PDF.
- For some Chinese papers: use **CNKI** or **ResearchGate** request.
- For hyperspectral satellite data: ask authors directly via email — these datasets are small (< 200 GB) and freely shared.
- For Earth observation foundation models: weights on **Hugging Face** (ibm-nasa-geospatial, clay-foundation, etc.).
